❓ How to use this app

This tool confirms a jack-up rig's as-set position on a platform. Work top-to-bottom in the left panel: import the CAD drawing, enter your Total Station shots, describe the rig, and the rig outline appears on the plot automatically. Everything runs offline in your browser.

1
Import the platform drawing (DXF). Section 1 → choose a .dxf file. If you only have .dwg, open it in CAD and "Save As" DXF first, and explode any blocks. Toggle layers on/off as needed.
2
Enter Total Station shots. Section 2 → pick input mode: Local X,Y, Range/Bearing, or Range only (laser). Click + Add Point per shot, type its values, press 📍 Bind on drawing and tap the matching feature (Snap helps land on exact corners/edges). You need ≥2 bound points.
3
Describe the rig. Section 3 → Length, Half-Width, Skid X/Y. The hull and skid envelope are drawn automatically at the fitted position.
4
Read the result. The Position Summary shows fitted Stern E/N, Heading and fit quality (RMSE). Enter Plan (4) / Navigation (5) to cross-check — the panel lists each pair's difference (Plan−TS, Nav−TS, Plan−Nav).
5
Export or save. Export CSV writes every parameter, summary and accuracy figure. Save Project reopens later (re-import the DXF). Clear / New Round resets all but keeps the Rig Profile.

Range only (laser) mode

What it does: if you can only shoot distances (no angles), the instrument position is trilaterated by least squares. Zero direction is fixed toward the Stern.
Heading is solved (not assumed) by shooting from 2 or more setup points on the rig, each with its own offset. Set the offsets in the "Setup offsets" list, then pick which Setup each shot belongs to.
Geometry: use ≥2 setups at different offsets, each with ≥2 shots (≥3 recommended), well spread out. With only 2 shots/setup a fit is still found, but if two poses fit almost equally the app asks you to add a shot or type an approx. heading to resolve it.

Coordinate convention

Local X, Y (section 2): +y = away from the rig (the way the instrument faces, opposite the Bow); +x = your right-hand side facing that way = the rig's Port side.
Instrument / Setup Offset (dx, dy): where the total station sits relative to Center Stern (0,0). Same +y/+x convention. A diamond ◆ marker appears for each so you can check it visually.

Plot controls

Pan: drag with mouse or one finger. Zoom: scroll wheel or pinch. Extents: fit everything in view.
Measure: tap 📏, then tap two points to read a distance. Add as many as you like; 🗑 clears them.
Snap (while binding) locks onto exact drawing vertices, edges, and circle/arc boundaries.

Tips

A point with a much larger residual than the others is highlighted ⚠ — re-check that shot or its binding.
If a Plan/Nav position is very far from the drawing, the app keeps the drawing in view and warns you (likely a typo).
Everything auto-saves in this browser; reopening restores your last session (except the DXF).
